Add your first library

Shortest path from install to browsing your movies. No Meteor account required — your movie files stay on your own PC or NAS.

Steps

  1. Install Meteor on your Android phone, tablet, or Android TV device.
  2. Connect that device to the same home network as your PC or NAS.
  3. Open Meteor and add a movie collection location. Not sure which type? Start with DLNA — details in Set up media access.
  4. Select the folder (or collection) that contains your movies.
  5. Start indexing. Meteor builds a searchable library on the device; your files stay on the PC/NAS.

Name your files clearly

Prefer a clear movie folder with sensible names. Cleaner names usually identify better. Meteor does not host your collection — it only indexes what you point it at.

Put the official title and year first, ideally as Title (YYYY). Tags for quality, audio, or edition after that are fine (for example 1080p, DTS, Director’s Cut). Avoid typos and personal notes that do not belong in the title — those often make Meteor pick the wrong movie, or none at all.

Helpful examples

  • The Matrix (1999).mkv
  • The Matrix (1999) 1080p DTS Director’s Cut.mkv
  • Casablanca (1942).mp4
  • Spirited Away (2001) Japanese.mkv

Less helpful examples

  • The Matrix - my old tape copy.mkv — personal notes confuse matching
  • Mattrix (1999).mkv — typos often miss the right title
  • The Matrix.mkv — missing year (harder when several films share a name)
  • Holiday movies from the attic.mkv — no real title or year

If a title is still wrong after indexing, use Pick a match or Search again in the app — you can choose the correct movie manually.

Need help choosing a source?

Use the comparison and setup guides under Set up media access (DLNA, SMB, WebDAV). Keep protocol details there — this page stays short on purpose.

Watch later without remote networking

At home, index your library, then use Download in Meteor for titles you want offline. You can play those downloads away from home without opening your network to the Internet. See Download as an easy alternative.

Having trouble?

If Meteor cannot find your server, or indexing does not start, check the basics on Help. Deeper networking topics (remote access, CGNAT, port forwarding) stay in advanced guides — you do not need them for a first DLNA library at home.
