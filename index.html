How it works

Three steps from install to watching.

  1. 1

    Install and connect

    Point Meteor at movies on your home network via DLNA, SMB, or WebDAV.

  2. 2

    Index

    Select your movie library and let Meteor identify and organize your collection on the device.

  3. 3

    Enjoy

    Browse, search, play — and download titles for offline viewing when you leave home.

What Meteor does

Focused on the movie-library experience — clean browsing, not every advanced player feature.

  • DLNA, SMB & WebDAV Connect collections the way your PC or NAS already shares them.
  • Browse and select folders Choose the movie folder Meteor should index.
  • Local indexing Build a searchable library on your device — no Meteor server.
  • Automatic movie identification Match titles so posters and details can appear.
  • Searchable personal library Find films quickly in your own collection.
  • Download for offline Save individual titles on the device to watch away from home.
  • Platform playback Uses your device’s media capabilities; VLC is a recommended fallback for tough files.
  • No Meteor account Use your library without signing up for Meteor.

Built for your collection

VLC is excellent at playing files, and services like OMDb can identify most movies. Meteor is for the missing piece: presenting your library nicely — without complicated server setups.

The aim is a good browsing experience for anyone with films on a NAS or PC — without needing advanced computer skills. At home, stream from your network. On the go, download titles in Meteor, or use remote WebDAV only if you choose that advanced path.

Playback depends on your device’s media stack and hardware decoding when available. We recommend the VLC app as a fallback for tougher files.
