What Meteor does

Focused on the movie-library experience — clean browsing, not every advanced player feature.

  • DLNA, SMB & WebDAV Connect collections the way your PC or NAS already shares them. No need to set up separate “server side” software beyond what you already use.
  • Browse and select folders Choose the movie folder Meteor should index.
  • Local indexing Build a searchable library on your device — your movie files stay on your own storage.
  • Automatic movie identification Match titles so posters and details can appear. Focus to pick the right match, or choose manually. When the title and year are clear in the filename, the correct movie is usually found.
  • Searchable personal library Find films quickly in your own collection.
  • Download for offline Save individual titles on the device to watch away from home on your phone or tablet.
  • Platform playback Uses your device’s media capabilities; VLC is a recommended fallback for tough files.
  • No Meteor account Meteor does not store your collection. It stays on your own devices.

Built for your collection

Meteor Media Portal aims to make your movies searchable and present them in a pleasant way — with a simple interface and without a complicated setup.

The first version focuses on that library experience, not on supporting every codec. Android devices often have strong hardware decoding, which is better for battery life and performance. My goal is to avoid picking the wrong titles and to keep the interface easy to use.

For files the device cannot decode natively, I recommend the excellent VLC app. Meteor can open the selected title in VLC, which supports more codecs.

In this release, movie identification primarily uses TMDB, with Wikimedia as support for some artwork and related media.
