What Meteor does

Focused on the movie-library experience — clean browsing, not every advanced player feature.

  • SMB, WebDAV & DLNA Connect movie collections on your LAN the way your NAS already shares them.
  • Browse and select folders Choose the movie folder Meteor should index.
  • Fast local indexing Build a searchable library on your device.
  • Automatic movie identification Match titles so posters and details can appear.
  • Searchable personal library Find films quickly in your own collection.
  • Posters and movie information Browse with artwork and descriptive metadata.
  • ExoPlayer playback Play with your device’s built-in media support; open the VLC app when a file needs it.
  • No Meteor account Use your library without signing up for Meteor.

Built for your collection

Meteor focuses on the part that makes a personal movie collection enjoyable: fast indexing, reliable identification, rich metadata and effortless browsing.

This version plays with ExoPlayer using your device’s built-in media support. It does not try to replace advanced players for every possible codec and format — when needed, Meteor can open the VLC app.
