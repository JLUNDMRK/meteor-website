1. What Meteor is

Meteor helps you connect to movie collections on your local network via SMB, WebDAV, or DLNA, index a library on your device, identify titles using third-party metadata services where configured, and browse and play movies using your device’s built-in media player.

Meteor does not provide movie files, images, or metadata of its own. You accept responsibility for OMDb API data (and other metadata providers) that you use, and for any movies or files you copy or download locally for use with the App. You are also responsible for ensuring you have the right to access and play that media.

2. Codecs & playback

This version of the App plays with your device’s built-in media player and the codecs that already come with the operating system. It does not embed extra codec engines such as FFmpeg or VLCKit. Some movies may not play in-app if the device cannot decode them.

When needed, Meteor can open the separate VLC app if you have installed it.
