Help

Answers for common questions. Sections expand in supporting browsers; all answers are visible in the page source without JavaScript.

Does Meteor require an account?

No. Meteor does not require a Meteor account to browse or play your local library. You may still need accounts for third-party services you choose to use elsewhere (for example your media server or Google Play).

Why can’t Meteor find my media server?

Local discovery often fails for network reasons. Check the following:

  • Your device and the media server must be on the same local network.
  • DLNA (or equivalent media sharing) must be enabled on the media server.
  • A video folder must be shared by the server.
  • VPN software can interfere with local discovery — try disabling it temporarily.
  • Router client isolation (AP isolation / guest Wi‑Fi) can prevent devices from seeing each other.
  • Firewalls on the NAS, PC, or router may block local discovery traffic.

Meteor cannot promise compatibility with every router, NAS, DLNA implementation, or device. If discovery still fails after these checks, see Contact.

Why was a movie identified incorrectly?

Identification uses filenames and folder names plus third-party metadata services. Ambiguous names, missing years, or incomplete provider data can produce the wrong match. Choose the correct title with a manual search in the app. If no good result appears, the movie may not be in the metadata provider’s database. See also Metadata & Attribution.

Which formats are supported?

Playback support depends on the device and OS first (AVPlayer / Media3). On Apple, Meteor may also use an embedded LGPL VLCKit engine (“Powered by VLC”) for containers the system player cannot open. On Android, Meteor does not embed VLC; it may open the external VLC app if you have installed it. Optional LGPL FFmpeg decode profiles may be used on some builds — see About for license details.

Does Meteor upload my movies?

Meteor is designed so that your movie files stay on your media server and are streamed for playback. Meteor does not upload your movie files to a Meteor server (there is no Meteor media backend for storing your library).

When movie identification runs, selected search terms (such as title and year derived from filenames) may be sent from your device to third-party metadata providers. See the Privacy Policy (draft) for details that still need confirmation against the shipped app.

Does Meteor need an Internet connection?

Local discovery and playback of files on your LAN do not require Internet access. An Internet connection is typically needed to identify movies and download posters and descriptive metadata from third-party providers. Exact offline behaviour should be confirmed against the shipped app before treating this as final.

Will Meteor always be free?

Future versions may offer different ways of accessing metadata, depending on operating costs and the terms of metadata providers.

Meteor is an independent project. My goal is to keep it affordable and accessible. If ongoing costs increase, optional subscriptions or other funding models may be introduced to help keep the project sustainable.

How do I report a problem?

Use the support email on the Contact page. Include your device model, Android version, media server type if known, and what you expected versus what happened.
