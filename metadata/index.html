How metadata works

Meteor may use third-party services to identify movies and retrieve descriptive metadata and artwork. Metadata can be incomplete, outdated or incorrect. Provider availability and terms can change.

Third-party names, artwork and data remain subject to their respective owners and licences.

Meteor aims to cache metadata and artwork on the device. It does not operate a central Meteor catalog that redistributes your library’s metadata to other users.

Incorrect matches

Automatic identification is a best-effort guess from filenames and folder names. If the wrong title is chosen, pick the correct movie with a manual search in the app. If nothing suitable appears, the title may simply not be in the metadata provider’s database — Meteor does not maintain that catalog, and there is no need to report individual mismatches.
