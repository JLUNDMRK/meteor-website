Privacy Policy

Draft effective date placeholder: 23 July 2026 — must be confirmed before publication.

Draft. This document is an initial project draft and must be reviewed against the final application behavior before publication.

Meteor (“the App”) is an independent Android application for browsing and playing movies from media servers on your local network. This policy describes, at a high level, how the App is intended to handle information. It does not invent behaviour that has not been confirmed in the shipped build.

Meteor does not require a Meteor account. The App is not designed to upload your movie files to a Meteor-operated media backend (there is no such backend for storing your library).

1. Local library and playback

When you add a movie collection location (SMB, WebDAV, or DLNA) and choose a folder, Meteor indexes movie items locally on the device so you can browse and search your library. Playback streams from your server using the device’s built-in media player. Meteor reads your collection; it does not host or rewrite your movie files on the NAS.

Confirm exact local storage contents (index schema, artwork cache, watch progress, settings) against the Android app before publication.

Confirm Android permissions requested (network, notifications, storage, etc.) and list them here.

2. Metadata identification

To identify movies and show posters and descriptive information, Meteor may send search terms derived from filenames and folder names (for example a title and year) from your device to third-party metadata providers configured for the build.

Those providers may receive technical information normally associated with an internet request (such as IP address, request time, and the query text). Over time, many queries from the same IP could suggest which titles were looked up. That is not the same as knowing your identity, but it is not fully anonymous.

Returned metadata and artwork references may be cached on the device so the App does not need to repeat every lookup while you browse.

Confirm exact metadata providers enabled in the published build (see also Metadata & Attribution and assets/site.config.js).

Confirm exact fields sent in metadata queries (title, year, identifiers, language, etc.).

Confirm whether any device or installation identifiers are included in metadata requests.

3. Analytics, crash reporting, and diagnostics

Confirm whether Firebase, Crashlytics, Play pre-launch reports, or any other analytics / crash / diagnostics SDK is included in the published build. If none are included, state that clearly. If any are included, describe what is sent and that library titles / file contents are not included — only if verified.

Until verified, this policy does not claim that “no data is collected.”

4. Support email

If you contact support, your message and email address are processed to respond to your request.

Confirm retention period for support email and whether messages are stored only in the mailbox or also elsewhere.

5. Children

The App is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Use is intended for people who control their own media libraries and devices.

6. Changes

This policy may be updated as the App evolves. The effective date above will be revised when a reviewed version is published.
