Privacy Policy

Effective date: 15 August 2026

Terms of Use Metadata & Attribution Contact

Meteor (“the App”) is an independent movie-library application for Android phones, tablets, and Android TV / Google TV. It helps you browse and play movies from a PC or NAS (for example via DLNA, SMB, or WebDAV), index a library on the device, optionally download titles for offline playback, and identify titles using third-party metadata services.

Meteor does not require a Meteor account and does not operate a Meteor media server that stores your movie files or library catalog. This policy describes how information is handled when you use the App. Related rules of use are in the Terms of Use.

1. Summary

  • Your library index, watch progress, settings, share credentials, cached artwork, and offline downloads stay on the device (or on your own PC/NAS for the original media files).
  • When you connect to a share, the App contacts your media servers on your network (or a remote WebDAV endpoint you configure) — not a Meteor content backend.
  • Movie identification may send filename-derived search terms to metadata providers such as OMDb (see Metadata & Attribution).
  • The Android build uses Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics for product usage and crash diagnostics.
  • Optional Supporter purchases use Google Play Billing. Google processes purchase data under Google’s terms.
  • Meteor does not sell your personal information and does not show third-party advertising in the App.

2. Information stored on your device

Depending on how you use the App, Meteor may store locally on the device:

  • Library index entries derived from your folders and media listings (paths, titles, technical file info).
  • Cached metadata and artwork references returned by identification providers.
  • Watch progress, favorites / lists, and app settings.
  • Credentials you enter for SMB, WebDAV, or similar access (stored with platform-appropriate protection on the device).
  • Media files you choose to Download for offline playback.
  • A local first-install timestamp used for the optional 90-day local trial of supporter features (reinstalling can reset this local trial clock).

Uninstalling the App removes app storage on that device, including local downloads stored in the App’s private storage. Media that remains on your PC or NAS is unaffected.

3. Network access to your media

To discover, browse, stream, or download from your collection, Meteor connects to the DLNA, SMB, WebDAV, or other endpoints you configure. Traffic goes between your device and those servers. Meteor does not upload your movie files to a Meteor-operated media backend (there is no such backend for hosting your library).

4. Metadata identification

To identify movies and show posters and descriptive information, Meteor may send search terms derived from filenames and folder names (for example a title and year), and related lookup identifiers when available, from your device to third-party metadata providers configured for the build. The free / personal build typically uses OMDb; other providers (such as TMDB) may be available depending on build and settings. Artwork may also be fetched from provider or Wikimedia-related sources when configured.

Those providers may receive technical information normally associated with an internet request (such as IP address, request time, and the query text). Over time, many queries from the same IP could suggest which titles were looked up. That is not the same as knowing your civil identity, but it is not fully anonymous.

Meteor aims to cache returned metadata and artwork on the device so lookups are not repeated unnecessarily. Meteor does not operate a central catalog that redistributes your library’s metadata to other users. Provider terms and availability can change; see Metadata & Attribution.

5. Analytics and crash reporting (Firebase)

The Android App includes Google Firebase services:

  • Firebase Analytics — app usage events (for example app opens and similar product metrics) to understand stability and feature use.
  • Firebase Crashlytics — crash and diagnostic reports (for example stack traces, app version, and device/OS information) to find and fix bugs.

These services are operated by Google. They may process device and app identifiers and diagnostic data according to Google’s Firebase / Google Play services documentation. Depending on platform version and SDK configuration, this can include an advertising ID where the platform makes one available. Meteor does not use advertising ID for ads, remarketing, or cross-app advertising, and the App does not include a third-party ads SDK.

Meteor is not designed to send your full movie file contents or NAS passwords to Firebase. Analytics events are kept intentionally limited; exact event names may evolve as the App improves. Do not treat Firebase as “no data collected” — diagnostics and usage metrics are collected when Firebase is active in the shipped build.

6. Purchases (Google Play Billing)

Optional tip-jar / Supporter features are offered through Google Play Billing (subscription). Google processes purchase tokens, order and subscription status, and related billing data under Google’s policies. Meteor receives enough purchase state to unlock supporter features on the device. Meteor does not receive your full payment card details.

Base browsing and playback features are intended to remain usable without a subscription; some features may be reserved for the local trial period or active supporters. See the Terms of Use and in-app wording for current entitlements.

7. Permissions (Android)

Depending on device and Android version, the App may request permissions such as network access (including Wi‑Fi multicast for discovery), notifications, and foreground service access used for downloads. Permissions are requested so the App can reach your media servers, notify you about downloads, and run download work reliably — not to scrape unrelated personal data from the device.

8. Support email

If you contact support or privacy at meteor@jlundmark.org, your message and email address are processed to respond to your request. Messages are handled in ordinary email systems used for that mailbox and retained as long as needed to handle the conversation and any follow-up, then deleted or archived according to normal mailbox practice. Do not send passwords in email if you can avoid it.

9. Children

The App is not directed at children and is not intended for use by children. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Use is intended for people who control their own media libraries and devices.

10. Sharing and processors

Meteor does not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by:

  • Google (Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, Google Play, Play Billing), under Google’s terms and privacy documentation.
  • Metadata and artwork providers you trigger through identification (for example OMDb), under those providers’ terms.
  • Your own PC, NAS, or remote WebDAV host when you connect the App to it.

European users: where GDPR or similar laws apply, processing is based on providing the App you requested (contract / legitimate interest in operating and improving a crash-aware product) and, for purchases, Google’s role as payment platform. This summary is not legal advice.

11. Your choices

  • You can limit network features by not adding remote shares and by using offline downloads only after content is already on the device.
  • You can clear app data or uninstall to remove local library state and credentials on that device.
  • You can manage Play subscriptions in Google Play.
  • Device-level advertising ID / app settings (where offered by Android) may affect what advertising identifiers third-party SDKs can read.
  • Privacy questions: use the contact below.

12. Changes

This policy may be updated as the App evolves. The effective date at the top will be revised when a new version is published. Continued use after an update means you should review the revised policy.
