Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2 October 2026

Terms of Use Metadata & Attribution Contact

Meteor (“the App”) is an independent movie-library application for Android phones, tablets, and Android TV / Google TV. I develop and publish Meteor as a solo project. It helps you browse and play movies from a PC or NAS (for example via DLNA, SMB, or WebDAV), index a library on the device, optionally download titles for offline playback, and identify titles using third-party metadata services.

Meteor does not require a Meteor account. Your movie files stay on your own storage (PC, NAS, or downloads on the device) — Meteor does not run a media host that stores or streams your film library. Optional app services (for example remote configuration from this website, or future optional sync of settings) are separate from hosting your movies. This policy describes how information is handled when you use the App. Related rules of use are in the Terms of Use.

1. Summary

  • Your library index, watch progress, settings, share credentials, cached artwork, and offline downloads stay on the device (or on your own PC/NAS for the original media files).
  • When you connect to a share, the App contacts your media servers on your network (or a remote WebDAV endpoint you configure) — not a Meteor content backend.
  • The App may fetch remote configuration / policy and open help or information pages from meteor.jlundmark.org (see below).
  • Movie identification may send filename-derived search terms to a metadata resolver service such as the TMDB or OMDb API (see Metadata & Attribution).
  • Meteor does not intentionally collect personal data such as your name, email, postal address, or a Meteor account profile. There is no Meteor login.
  • The Android build uses Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics (Google) for limited product metrics and crash diagnostics — not for ads or marketing profiles.
  • Those Google SDKs may read a device advertising ID where Android makes one available. Meteor does not use it to identify you as a person, show ads, or build a marketing profile.
  • The current public release has no in-app purchases or subscriptions. Google Play Billing code is not used to start purchases in this build.
  • I do not sell your personal information. Meteor does not show third-party advertising in the App.

2. Information stored on your device

Depending on how you use the App, Meteor may store locally on the device:

  • Library index entries derived from your folders and media listings (paths, titles, technical file info).
  • Cached metadata and artwork references returned by identification providers.
  • Watch progress, favorites / lists, and app settings.
  • Credentials you choose to save for SMB, WebDAV, or similar access. On Android these are stored on the device using the platform’s normal secure storage approach (Android Keystore for key material, with ciphertext kept in app-private storage). In the current release they are not uploaded to meteor.jlundmark.org or used to host your movies elsewhere.
  • Media files you choose to Download for offline playback.
  • A local first-seen / first-install timestamp that may be used for product analytics or future early-adopter eligibility. It does not lock features after 90 days in the current free-during-development release.

Uninstalling the App removes app storage on that device, including local downloads stored in the App’s private storage. Media that remains on your PC or NAS is unaffected.

Security recommendation: Prefer a separate account with read-only access limited to the media folders Meteor needs — not an admin account, write access, or access to unrelated or sensitive files. Treat share credentials like any other password you store in an app on your phone.

Meteor is designed to access your media without modifying or deleting files on your server. However, as with any software accessing a media library, I recommend keeping backups of files you do not want to lose.

3. Network access to your media

To discover, browse, stream, or download from your collection, Meteor connects to the DLNA, SMB, WebDAV, or other endpoints you configure. Traffic goes between your device and those servers. Meteor does not upload your movie files to a Meteor-operated media host — film data stays on your storage (or on the device if you use Download).

4. App configuration and this website

Meteor may fetch remote app configuration / settings / policy from meteor.jlundmark.org (for example which metadata provider is active, and related operational settings). That request is used to configure the App; it is not a login to a Meteor account and is not used to host or index your movie files.

The App may also open help, getting-started, legal, and other information pages on this website (for example Help, Getting Started, Privacy, Terms, and Metadata & Attribution) when you choose those links in the App.

Visiting those pages works like ordinary web browsing. The configuration fetch itself is intended to retrieve signed policy / configuration for the App, not to upload your library catalog or share passwords.

5. Metadata identification

To identify movies and show posters and descriptive information, Meteor may send search terms derived from filenames and folder names (for example a title and year), and related lookup identifiers when available, from your device to a metadata resolver service such as the TMDB or OMDb API. Artwork and related descriptive data may also be fetched from Wikidata / Wikimedia-related sources when configured. See Metadata & Attribution.

Meteor uses a metadata resolver service such as the TMDB or OMDb API for accurate titles and metadata. Users are encouraged to support the configured provider — for example by contributing to the project or by using their own API key where that option is offered.

Those services may receive technical information normally associated with an internet request (such as IP address, request time, and the query text). Over time, many queries from the same IP could suggest which titles were looked up. That is not the same as knowing your civil identity, but it is not fully anonymous.

Meteor aims to cache returned metadata and artwork on the device so lookups are not repeated unnecessarily. Meteor does not operate a central catalog that redistributes your library’s metadata to other users. Provider terms and availability can change.

6. Analytics and crash reporting (Firebase)

Meteor does not ask for or collect classic personal contact details (name, email, phone, postal address) and does not create a Meteor user account. Library contents and share passwords stay on your device / your storage — they are not uploaded to Firebase for identity or advertising.

The Android App includes Google Firebase services for product quality only:

  • Firebase Analytics — limited app usage events (for example app opens and similar product metrics) to understand stability and feature use.
  • Firebase Crashlytics — crash and diagnostic reports (for example stack traces, app version, and device/OS information) to find and fix bugs.

These services are operated by Google. They may process technical device and app identifiers and diagnostic data according to Google’s Firebase / Google Play services documentation. Depending on platform version and SDK configuration, that can include an advertising ID where Android makes one available. That identifier is a resettable device/app identifier used by Google’s diagnostics stack — it is not your name or civil identity, and Meteor cannot use it to identify you as a person. Meteor does not use advertising ID for ads, remarketing, cross-app advertising, or marketing profiles, and the App does not include a third-party ads SDK.

Analytics events are kept intentionally limited; exact event names may evolve as the App improves. Do not treat Firebase as “zero data” — technical diagnostics and limited usage metrics are processed by Google when Firebase is active in the shipped build — but Meteor is not designed to build a personal profile of you from that data.

7. Purchases (current release)

The current public Meteor release is free during active development. There is no reachable in-app purchase or subscription flow. Meteor does not initiate Google Play Billing purchases in this build, and Google does not process Meteor purchase data for this release because no purchase is started.

Future versions may introduce optional paid features. If that happens, this policy will be updated and the applicable app version will describe what is billed and by whom. See the Terms of Use.

8. Permissions (Android)

Depending on device and Android version, the App may request permissions such as network access (including Wi‑Fi multicast for discovery), notifications, and foreground service access used for downloads. Permissions are requested so the App can reach your media servers, notify you about downloads, and run download work reliably — not to scrape unrelated personal data from the device.

9. Support email

If you contact me about support or privacy at meteor@jlundmark.org, your message and the email address you send from are processed only so I can respond. Messages are handled in ordinary email systems for that mailbox and retained as long as needed for the conversation, then deleted or archived according to normal mailbox practice.

Please do not include personal data in support mail (for example full name beyond what your email address already shows, national ID numbers, photos of IDs, private addresses, or share/NAS passwords). Meteor is not set up as a personal-data helpdesk and I do not want to receive or store that information. Describe the technical problem (device model, Android version, media server type if known, steps to reproduce) without attaching credentials or unnecessary personal details. If you send such data anyway, I may ask you to resend a redacted message and will delete the sensitive content when practical.

10. Children

The App is not directed at children and is not intended for use by children. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Use is intended for people who control their own media libraries and devices.

11. Sharing and processors

I do not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by:

  • Google (Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, Google Play distribution), under Google’s terms and privacy documentation. Play Billing is not used for purchases in the current free release.
  • Metadata and artwork providers you trigger through identification (for example TMDB or OMDb), under those providers’ terms.
  • Your own PC, NAS, or remote WebDAV host when you connect the App to it.
  • This website (meteor.jlundmark.org) when the App fetches remote configuration or when you open help / information pages.

European users (GDPR and similar laws): Meteor does not intentionally collect personal data to identify you (no Meteor account; no request for name, national ID, or similar as part of normal app use). Technical processing for Firebase diagnostics / limited analytics (including, where applicable, an advertising ID read by Google’s SDKs) and metadata lookups you trigger is based on providing the App you requested and legitimate interest in operating a stable, crash-aware product. That technical data is not used by Meteor to identify you as a named individual. Support email is voluntary — please avoid sending personal data (see §9); any message you send is processed only to reply. This summary is not legal advice.

12. Your choices

  • You can clear app data or uninstall to remove local library state and credentials on that device.
  • Device-level advertising ID / app settings (where offered by Android) may affect what advertising identifiers SDKs can read.
  • Privacy questions: use the contact below.

13. Changes

I may update this policy as the App evolves. The effective date at the top will be revised when a new version is published. Continued use after an update means you should review the revised policy.
